Six-Layer PCB Architecture

Hardware stages in the SYSTEM-3 flight stack

SYSTEM-3 uses a vertical six-board stack. Each stage occupies a 95 × 95 mm deck and supports a distinct subsystem from science payload through antenna deployment.

Stage 1

Science Payload Deck

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • CsI(Tl) scintillator crystal (50×50×3 mm)
  • Hamamatsu SiPM array (C3015822)

Stage 2

Analog Front-End

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • LTC6227 low-noise op-amp
  • CLC high-frequency pi noise filter

Stage 3

Core Flight Computer

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • STM32F411RET6 100 MHz ARM core
  • EXTI10 background interrupts

Stage 4

EPS Power Manager

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • Texas Instruments BQ25713 buck-boost
  • I2C MPPT solar charger

Stage 5

Battery Ballast Floor

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • Dual 18650 LiFePO4 series cells
  • RFM95W UHF transceiver

Stage 6

Antenna Deployment Deck

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • 16.3 cm spring-steel tape-measure dipole
  • 10 Ω 1 W thermal burn actuator
